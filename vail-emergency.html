Skawak Creek Fire

GO: Evacuation Order In Effect

Size
~4,400 acres
Power
Shut off in affected areas
Highway
I-70 westbound closed
Direction
Evacuate to the EAST
Mandatory evacuation is in effect from West Vail to the golf course. Evacuate east. Westbound routes are closed. Follow directions from police and posted signage.

Critical instructions

  • Leave now if you are in an evacuation zone. Do not delay.
  • Do not return to evacuated areas for any reason, including pets or belongings.
  • Shelter-in-place locations, including designated parking structures, may be available if you cannot leave. Follow official direction.
  • Hikers and campers returning from trails: check in with authorities at staffed intersections before attempting to drive out.
Offers of help: Please do not drop off supplies or contact emergency lines with donation offers. To help, coordinate through the American Red Cross.
