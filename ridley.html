Voice

The visual identity promises calm clarity; the words have to keep that promise. Plain language, short sentences, the patient always addressed directly. Never clinical distance, never false cheer.

Never this

"Patient education materials indicate that benign prostatic hyperplasia affects approximately 50% of males over the age of 50. Treatment modalities may include pharmacological intervention."

Always this

"About half of men over fifty have this. It is common, it is treatable, and it is not cancer. Your doctor wants to start with a medication, and here is what it does."
