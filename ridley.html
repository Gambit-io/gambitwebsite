The brand

The companion is the dot on the i

It is the only place the wordmark breaks pattern, and it is the whole brand in one gesture: a bright point of presence, always beside the patient. It breathes at a resting rate, five seconds per cycle. Wherever the dot appears, it means the same thing: Ridley is with you.

Why this holds · the three locks
01 · No invented questions
Ridley reads from a registry of clinically approved questions. It cannot invent new ones.
02 · Deterministic routing
Outcomes are reached through registry-defined branching logic. Not AI judgment.
03 · Locked clinical wording
Verbatim delivery of the clinic's approved phrasing. Ridley never paraphrases clinical content.
Three condition pathways live today: BPH, erectile dysfunction, and microscopic hematuria. Each built on accepted instruments (IPSS, SHIM) and each ending in an outcome plus a SOAP note.
