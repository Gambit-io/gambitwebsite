The loop

The same five moves, every target.

A name and a website is enough. Bogan runs the loop on every signal, so the desk only sees the targets worth a partner's time.

01

Read the signal

Ownership changes, liquidity events, founder transitions, registry matches. Public signal in, no list bought.

02

Derive the pattern

Reads your portfolio and past deals to learn how you actually buy, not just the mandate you wrote down.

03

Score the target

Grades each target against that pattern. Taxonomic precision, so a manufacturer is never scored as a servicer.

04

Draft the opener

Writes the first touch and the investment brief behind it, in your voice, grounded in primary sources.

05

Rank the field

Reads the competitive picture and ranks where you stand against other likely buyers, so you move on the ones you can win.
