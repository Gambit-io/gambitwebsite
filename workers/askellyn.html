01 · Built on lived experience

Built on a life, not a dataset.

AskEllyn is built on the lived experience of Ellyn Winters-Robinson, a breast cancer survivor and author of Flat Please, Hold the Shame. Trained on real patient conversations and Ellyn's own narrative, the warmth is not a script. It is a person who has been there, at scale.

  • Grounded in one survivor's real journey
  • Trained on real patient conversations
  • Warmth that only lived experience carries
02 · Any language, any hour

Warm, in her own words, whenever she needs it.

Patients arrive at any hour, in any language, carrying fear that is hard to say out loud. AskEllyn answers with empathy first, in the language that feels most natural, so nobody has to wait for business hours or a translator to be heard.

  • 50+ languages, the patient's own words
  • Any hour, when the clinic is closed
  • Free to the patient, no appointment

03 · Safe by design

A companion, not a clinician.

AskEllyn knows exactly what it is not. It never diagnoses and never prescribes, and it says so plainly. It is emotional support that meets patients with warmth, then keeps the clinician firmly in charge of the care.

  • Never diagnoses, never prescribes
  • Clear disclaimers, support not advice
  • Clinical questions handed to the care team
