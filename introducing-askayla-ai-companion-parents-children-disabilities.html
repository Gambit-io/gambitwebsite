Introducing AskAyla: The World's First AI Companion for Parents Raising a Child with a Disability

Alex Bukowski
April 2026

Gambit Co is proud to introduce AskAyla, the world's first AI companion built specifically for parents and caregivers raising a child with a disability.

No Parent Should Ever Feel Alone

Millions of families around the world are raising a child with a disability, and so many of them are doing it alone. The grief that follows a diagnosis is real and complex, and for too long there has been nowhere to turn that truly understood what these families were going through. AskAyla was built to change that.

It was built by parents raising a child with severe quadriplegic cerebral palsy, infused with their lived experience and shaped by a deep belief that no parent should ever feel alone on this journey.

A Companion That Truly Understands

Parents and caregivers can visit askayla.org, type anything on their mind and receive a response that speaks directly to what they are going through. Whether that is a question about therapies, funding, school systems or the emotions that are hardest to say out loud to anyone in their lives, AskAyla listens without judgment and responds with warmth, honesty and the kind of understanding that only comes from lived experience.

Designed as a companion, not a clinician, AskAyla does not diagnose or replace a healthcare team. It is there to support parents and caregivers through the emotional weight of raising a child with a disability — from the earliest days of a diagnosis to navigating therapies and finding community, to planning for their child's future.

Forever Free for Every Family

AskAyla is forever free for every family that needs it, with no subscriptions, no paywalls and no barriers. Every parent and caregiver raising a child with a disability deserves access to real support, any time, regardless of where they live or what they can afford.

AskAyla is live now and available 24/7 at askayla.org.
