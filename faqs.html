FAQs

Answers to the questions we hear most. For everything else, Ask Gambit.

General

We build and deploy AI workers that do real jobs inside your business. Not chatbots. Not prototypes. Workers with a defined role, your brand's voice, and accountability to outcomes. They handle customer calls, process freight documents, coach employees, and support patients, whatever the job requires.

A chatbot answers questions. An AI worker does the job. Each Gambit worker has a defined role, a scope, and accountability to an outcome, and it runs the work end to end: reading the documents, running the process, drafting the output, and handing off to a person when it should. Copilots wait to be prompted and agents run loose scripts. A worker owns a job and is measured on results. Read the full explainer.

We build, not advise. Most AI consultancies deliver slide decks. We deliver working products that are live, measured, and improving every week. Our workers are deployed across financial services, healthcare, government, logistics, legal, hospitality, and more.
