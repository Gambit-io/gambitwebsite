We Took Two AI Workers to Vegas

Alex Bukowski
June 2026

Last week, Ryan (our Creative Director) and Pat (our CEO) headed to HPE Discover in Las Vegas. But not by themselves, of course.

Ryan brought Chloe. Pat brought Bogan. Neither of them needed a plane ticket or a hotel room, so they made pretty easy travel companions.

For those who haven't met them yet, Chloe and Bogan are AI workers we've built for two very different organizations. Think of them as digital teammates with specific jobs to do.

Chloe works for the Town of Vail, helping residents and visitors get information about parking and events. Bogan works for Spearhead Corporate Development, where he helps the mergers and acquisitions team research acquisition targets, write personalized outreach, and manage follow-up communication.

Chloe Worked the HPE Booth

Ryan spent the week introducing attendees to Chloe, who spent most of her time hanging out at the HPE booth.

Chloe helps residents and visitors get quick answers about parking and events in Vail. She can also communicate in more than 50 languages, which is one of those things we obviously had to test.

Sidenote: I (yes, me, Alex) decided to see how well Chloe handled Polish. Since Polish is my first language, I figured I'd have a decent shot at catching her if she made a mistake.

I called her, texted her, and spent a few minutes trying to catch her off guard.

Chloe handled everything beautifully (she's annoyingly good at this).

Back to Vegas: Ryan told me there were a lot of phones out at the HPE booth that week as attendees called and texted Chloe for themselves. He absolutely loved seeing that.

Bogan Took the Stage

Pat wasn't the only one talking about Bogan.

Gambit's crew was joined by Cameron Vernest, CEO of Spearhead Corporate Development, along with other members of the Spearhead team. Since Bogan works alongside Spearhead's mergers and acquisitions team today, attendees had the chance to hear directly from the people working with him.

Bogan helps Spearhead's mergers and acquisitions team identify acquisition targets, conduct research, write personalized outreach, and manage follow-up communication.

Having the Spearhead team there was great because attendees could hear the story from their perspective. Cameron and the team shared how they're using Bogan and answered questions throughout the session.

The Best Part of the Week

Like most conferences, HPE Discover involved a lot of walking, a lot of conversations, and probably more coffee than we'd like to admit.

It also gave us the chance to introduce Chloe and Bogan to people who hadn't met them before, catch up with customers and partners, and spend time talking about the projects we're building.

That's always one of the best parts for our team.

Back to Work

A big thank you to everyone who stopped by to chat with us, meet Chloe, learn more about Bogan, and share what you're working on. We had a blast.

If we're back at HPE Discover next time, we might just bring Chloe and Bogan along again. They travel pretty light.

And who knows? The next AI worker we build might be for your team. If you'd like to explore what that could look like, get in touch with us.
