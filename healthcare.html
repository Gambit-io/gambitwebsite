In the clinic

A specialist consult that writes itself up.

In a pilot with a hospital specialist clinic, a Gambit worker reads the referral, runs the structured consultation with the patient, shows the clinician its reasoning as it goes, and drafts the clinical note for review. The clinician stays in charge. The write-up stops piling up.
