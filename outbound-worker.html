Case study

One county. Two weeks. Zero dollars.

Spearhead · Florida · Origination
The challenge

Find business owners likely to sell, in a market no list covers, without spending a dollar on data.

What Bogan did

Ingested one county's public divorce filings, joined them to the state business registry, verified the owners, and drafted outreach for each.

The result

Twelve verified owners with a reason to sell, contacted the same fortnight. Nothing left the box. Fully repeatable, in any county.
