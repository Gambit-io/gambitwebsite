What Ita is

Meet Ita

Ita is one worker with one brain. She starts as a single tool and grows, over time, into a full suite. Named for a HOEMie the team already knew, so she arrives warm and familiar, not a piece of software.

Here is how she is put together.

The architecture

One spine. Many applications.

Everything Ita knows lives in one place. Each application draws only the part it needs.

Add an application and it inherits the brain. Add a channel and it just carries her. Nothing gets rebuilt.

The order, on purpose

Earn trust inside,
then face students

Ita starts on staff tools, where a person checks her work and the stakes are known. Once she is reliable there, she turns outward: answering students, guiding bookings, picking up the phone. Trust is earned in the back office before it is spent at the front desk.
