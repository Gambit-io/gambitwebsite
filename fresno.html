03 · Colour: the law

Loud in the moments. Quiet in the work.

A property platform still has to show someone a delinquency report. So the brightness has rules. It lives in motion, moments, and one button. The work surface stays ink on chalk.

Where the color lives

  • Loading, transitions, celebrations: the stripes own motion.
  • Chili = the one primary action per screen. Never two.
  • Sunset & Gold = highlights, active states, tiny accents.
  • Pacific = positive signals: paid, resolved, occupied.

Where it doesn't

  • Tables, ledgers, forms: ink on chalk. Numbers never wear stripes.
  • Red means overdue, so brand chili never sits next to alert red.
  • Backgrounds stay warm chalk, never bright colors.
  • If everything is loud, nothing is. One pop per screen.
