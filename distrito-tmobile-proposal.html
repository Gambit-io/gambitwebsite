Portfolio Intelligence Dashboard

A dashboard that brings restaurant, park, hotel, parking, foot traffic, and event data from 78 source files into one place, with a way to trace every number back to its source and an assistant that can answer questions about it.

Today, seeing the full picture means going through 78 separate reports across six systems by hand. This dashboard brings them together automatically and keeps them current.

What this gives you
  • One place to see restaurants, Toroverde Park, hotels, parking, foot traffic, and events, instead of 78 separate reports
  • Hotel numbers that read the same way across three different PMS systems
  • Every number traceable back to its source report
  • Distrito AI, to answer questions about the data directly
Our approach
  1. Harden and normalize the data pipeline
  2. Automate how reports come in
  3. Launch and hand off
What this becomes

This isn't a one-time delivery. Pat walked through this same progression on the call, and the monthly fee is what builds it out, step by step:

  • A dedicated inbox, watched automatically. Properties keep emailing reports exactly as they do today, to one address, and the dashboard grabs and loads them with no new habit for your team.
  • A missing-data monitor. If a property hasn't sent its report, the system notices and can send the reminder itself, before it becomes a gap in the dashboard.
  • Direct system hooks, per property. Hooking directly into each property's own system, such as Aloha, the PMS platforms, or Intercard, so the data arrives without anyone needing to send or upload it.
  • A permissions layer as more data joins. As more contacts and sources get added, especially anything touching HR, who sees what gets set up on purpose, not left as a default.
  • Distrito AI going proactive. Once the data is reliably in one place, it could start reaching outward on its own, for example flagging a property that's off pace and drafting a note about it.

Each step builds on the last, so the relationship keeps getting more useful the longer it runs, not less.
